Telegram Bot Interactive Commands

You can send any of the following commands to your Telegram Bot anytime from 7 AM to 7 PM:

/present_all or all present
Instantly marks all active plant employees as Present for today.
/attendance
Returns live attendance breakdown (Present, Absent, Half-day, and names).
/wages
Returns today's production wages & loading/unloading wages summary.
/status or /today
Returns full daily plant status (inward, outward, dispatches, worker count).
/morning
Triggers the 7:00 AM Morning Shift Start reminder preview.
/evening
Triggers the 7:00 PM Evening Work Completed recap preview.
